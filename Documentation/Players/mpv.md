- [1] AV1 is only offered on devices with hardware AV1 decoding (A17 Pro, M3, or newer).
- Embedded subtitle support is FFmpeg's known set; mpv does not report subtitle decoders.
- Unlisted external text subtitles are converted to the first external format by the server.
